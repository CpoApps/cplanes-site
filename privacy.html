PRIVACY POLICY / EFFECTIVE 17 NOVEMBER 2026

A clear view
of your data.

cPlanes is developed by Sipke Lautenbach / CpoApps. No CpoApps account is required to play.

Data on your device

The app stores game progress, best scores and preferences locally so it can remember your settings and achievements. It also creates a random installation identifier to merge handled-flight totals between devices; this is not an advertising identifier. Gameplay simulation runs on your device. The app does not request your location, microphone, camera, contacts or photos.

Optional iCloud

When iCloud is available and enabled, eligible game progress, handled-flight totals and the associated random installation identifier can synchronize between devices using Apple’s private iCloud key-value storage under your Apple account. Apple operates this service. CpoApps does not operate a server to receive your progress. Sync depends on your Apple account, device settings and network connection.

Optional Game Center

Game Center can authenticate your Apple gaming profile and submit scores to configured leaderboards. Your Game Center display name, scores and leaderboard position may be visible to other players according to Apple’s features and your privacy settings. You can continue local play without signing in. Manage Game Center through Apple’s settings.

No advertising or tracking

The current app includes no advertising, tracking SDKs, third-party analytics or developer-operated accounts. CpoApps does not sell personal information or use gameplay data for targeted advertising. Apple may process downloads, purchases if applicable, optional diagnostics and account information under Apple’s privacy policy.

TestFlight and diagnostics

If you test a beta through TestFlight, Apple may share testing information, crash reports and feedback you choose to send with the developer. This is handled by TestFlight and your Apple settings. Please review screenshots before submitting feedback.

Support correspondence

If you contact cpoapps@gmail.com, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send so we can respond. Send only what is needed. You may request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any required recordkeeping. Public GitHub issues are visible to others.

Control and deletion

Use the app’s settings to reset local progress and manage available sync options. Removing the app removes its local app data, subject to Apple device backup behavior. A local reset or uninstall may not remove records already held by iCloud or Game Center. Manage those services and their data through Apple’s account and device settings.

This website

This static site has no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels or contact form. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process request information such as IP addresses for hosting and security under GitHub’s privacy statement. Email and external websites have their own policies.

Children

The app does not ask players to provide personal information. Apple account and Game Center participation are governed by Apple’s account rules and parental controls. Avoid including children’s personal information in feedback or public support posts.

Contact and changes

Send privacy questions or support-data deletion requests to cpoapps@gmail.com. If the app’s data practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date.

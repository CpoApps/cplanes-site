On your frequency

How do I start?

Choose Area Control or Airfield Control, select a scenario and begin with the lowest difficulty. Select an aircraft on the radar or flight strip, then use its available commands. Pause whenever you need time to plan.

What is Area Control?

Keep aircraft separated horizontally and vertically. Use heading, target altitude, speed and direct-to instructions to guide each flight toward its assigned route or exit. Check waypoint altitude restrictions before handing a flight off.

What is Airfield Control?

Manage arrivals and departures around an airfield. Align arrivals, descend and slow them, then issue an approach clearance when the flight meets the approach requirements. Release departures only when the runway is available. Use a go-around when a landing sequence cannot safely continue.

Why did I receive a warning?

A warning can indicate a separation conflict, a route or altitude restriction, weather exposure, runway conflict, low fuel or an unstable approach. Read the flight details and event messages, then adjust the clearance. Vertical separation matters even when radar symbols are close together.

Can I pause or rotate?

Yes. Use the pause control to stop the simulation. Both game modes adapt to portrait and landscape on iPhone and iPad. The simulation pauses when the app becomes inactive so an interruption does not silently consume your shift.

Which languages are supported?

The interface supports English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese. Select your preferred supported language in the app settings. Aviation callsigns and waypoint identifiers remain unchanged.

How does iCloud progress work?

The game keeps progress on your device. With iCloud enabled and available, eligible progress can also synchronize through Apple’s private iCloud key-value storage using the same Apple account. Sync can take time and requires network access. No CpoApps account is required.

How does Game Center work?

Game Center is optional. Sign in with your Apple account when prompted to use configured leaderboards. If you are offline, not signed in, or a beta leaderboard is not configured yet, local play remains available. Simulator testing cannot guarantee that a leaderboard is live.

Can I delete my progress?

Use the progress reset control in the app settings. If cloud sync is enabled, follow the reset information shown in the app; a local reset alone may not remove progress already stored in iCloud. Apple account and Game Center information are managed through Apple’s settings.

How can I report a problem?

Email cpoapps@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, app version, scenario and a short description of what happened. TestFlight testers can also use Send Beta Feedback. Do not include passwords or information you would not want shared in a screenshot.
